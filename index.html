Professional Profile

Dr. Zahir Edrees is an accomplished academic, researcher and higher education leader with extensive expertise in Software Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Natural Language Processing, Information Retrieval and Machine Learning.


He holds a Ph.D. in Computer Engineering and has significant experience in teaching, curriculum development, scientific research, academic administration and digital transformation initiatives.


His research interests span Generative AI, Large Language Models, intelligent information retrieval, healthcare informatics, educational technology and intelligent decision-support systems.
